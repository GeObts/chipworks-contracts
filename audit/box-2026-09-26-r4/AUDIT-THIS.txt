WHAT CHANGED SINCE ROUND 3
Details: section R4 of the brief and TRIAGE-ROUND3.md (paste 3).

1. BOX-R3-L1 (fixed): the refused-transfer cap no longer strands a payable box.
   - The cap now applies only inside the Pyth callback. settle() takes a new flag, gasBounded:
     true from the callback, false from claimOwed.
   - claimOwed is an ordinary transaction, so it walks every stock and then USDC. A box sent to OWED by
     two refusals is paid by the next stock that accepts it.
   - The cap is deliberately not tied to gasleft(): on the revealWithCallback recovery path the caller
     picks the gas, so gasleft() would let them choose between stock and USDC.
   - Box is now 23,801 bytes.

2. Worst-case callback gas, measured on the live node instead of estimated
   (gas/box-refusing-opener-sim.cjs and its .out.txt, paste 4).
   - The opener is an OFAC-listed address. Both B20 policy and Circle's USDC refuse transfers to it.
   - 13 stocks listed, 10 of them funded by real restocks.
   - Result: 580,918 gas, with 2 real refused transfers, USDC refused, and the box going OWED.
   - Estimated about 680k at MAX_STOCKS = 16, against the 900k floor.
