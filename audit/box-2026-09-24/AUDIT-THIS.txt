AUDIT REQUEST, ROUND 2: ChipWorks Box (Box.sol, PrizeVault.sol, ChipConverter.sol; Solidity 0.8.24, Base mainnet, not yet deployed)

What it is: sealed, giftable ERC-721 gacha boxes ($1/$10/$25, paid in USDC or $CHIP). Opening requests Pyth
Entropy v2; the callback draws a prize from an on-chain odds table (91% RTP) and a prize vault pays it in a
tokenized stock (USDC fallback). Prizes are never $CHIP. Unpayable prizes are never paid short: the box becomes
"owed" at the exact prize. A keeper restocks the vault with stocks; a permissionless sweep sends surplus to the
fee recipient.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE ROUND 1 (details: section R of the brief, and TRIAGE-ROUND1.md in paste 3):
- Round 1 confirmed a fixed-$CHIP-price arbitrage (a "$10" box funded the pool $4.75 when $CHIP halved). $CHIP
  buys now SWAP TO THE EXACT USDC PRICE INSIDE THE BUY (the deployed ChipLottery's exact-output route). The keeper
  $CHIP sale, its caps, the owner price floor and the $CHIP recovery are all gone.
- Entropy requests are keyed by (provider, sequence); the coverage gate runs before any payment.

Attack hardest:
1. THE NEW $CHIP BUY: can anyone make a $CHIP buy mint a box while the pool gets less than 95% of the price, leave
   tokens on the converter or Box, take more from a buyer than their own swap spent, or re-enter mid-buy? Is the
   v4 exact-output swap and settle correct?
2. THE PYTH CALLBACK: can anyone make it exceed its gas limit (measured ~466k-574k with real B20s against
   1,000,000; the stock list is capped at 16) or revert? Can an opener choose or re-roll an outcome? Is the F-2
   fix (provider-keyed requests) complete?
3. THE OWED PATH and SOLVENCY: can an owed prize be paid twice, to the wrong address, or at the wrong size? Can
   restock, the sweep or the owner take the pool below what sold boxes are owed or could win?
