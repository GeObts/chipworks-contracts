AUDIT REQUEST: ChipWorks Box (Box.sol, PrizeVault.sol, ChipConverter.sol; Solidity 0.8.24, Base mainnet, not yet deployed)

What it is: sealed ERC-721 gacha boxes ($1/$10/$25, paid in USDC or $CHIP). Opening requests Pyth Entropy v2;
the callback draws a prize from an on-chain odds table (91% RTP) and a prize vault pays it in a tokenized stock
(USDC fallback). Prizes are never $CHIP. A $CHIP payment goes whole to ChipConverter, which a keeper sells for
USDC (5% fee / 95% vault). A keeper restocks the vault with stocks; a permissionless sweep sends surplus to the
fee recipient. Unpayable prizes are never paid short: the box becomes "owed" at the exact prize.

Attack hardest:
1. THE ONE KEEPER TRUST POINT: $CHIP has no on-chain price, so ChipConverter.sellChip's $CHIP leg is bounded only
   by the keeper's minUsdcOut plus caps and an owner floor. Can the keeper (or anyone) send value anywhere but the
   fee recipient and the vault, exceed the caps, or bypass the floor? Is the Uniswap v4 unlock/settle correct?
2. THE PYTH CALLBACK: can anyone make it exceed its gas limit or revert (we measured ~466k-573k with real B20s
   against a 1,000,000 limit; the vault's stock list is capped at 16 as a gas bound and the limit cannot be set
   below 900,000)? Can an opener choose or re-roll an outcome (retryOpen is gated on the Pyth request never having
   been revealed and a 30-day timeout)?
3. THE OWED PATH: can an owed prize be paid twice, to the wrong address, or at the wrong size? Is the liability
   accounting exact on every path? Can someone force a prize into "owed" for gain?
4. SOLVENCY: can restock, the sweep, or the owner take the pool below what sold boxes are owed or could win?

For each finding: severity, file and line, the exact calls that exploit it, and a fix. If you find nothing on a
question, say so for that question. The full brief (15 questions) is the first file of paste 1; the contracts are pastes 1 and 2.

Before any findings, fill in the receipt table from section 0 of the brief for every file, so we know nothing
was cut off.
